Account Security Policy

All accounts are required to use a password of at least 10 characters,
including at least one number and one symbol. Passwords are hashed using
a salted algorithm before storage; the platform never stores or can
retrieve a user's plaintext password under any circumstance.

Two-factor authentication (2FA) can be enabled from the security settings
page using either an authenticator app or SMS. Accounts handling payment
information above $500 in stored balance are required to enable 2FA; the
platform will restrict withdrawal actions on such accounts until 2FA is
configured.

If suspicious login activity is detected, such as a login from a new
device in a different country within a short time window, the account is
automatically locked and the user is notified by email and, if configured,
SMS. The account owner must verify their identity through a support ticket
before the lock is lifted; this process typically takes 1 to 3 business
days.

Session tokens expire after 30 days of inactivity, or immediately upon a
password change, which logs the user out of all active sessions on every
device. API keys issued for third-party integrations follow a separate
90-day rotation policy and must be regenerated manually; the platform does
not send a reminder before an API key expires.

Deleted accounts enter a 30-day recovery window during which the account
can be restored by contacting support. After 30 days, all associated data,
including order history and stored payment methods, is permanently and
irreversibly deleted in compliance with data retention regulations.
