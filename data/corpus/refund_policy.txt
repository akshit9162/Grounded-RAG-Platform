Refund Policy

Standard orders placed on the platform are eligible for a full refund within
30 days of delivery, provided the item is returned in its original condition
with all packaging intact. Refunds are processed to the original payment
method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item is received and
inspected by our warehouse team.

Digital goods, including gift cards and downloadable software licenses, are
non-refundable once the associated activation code has been redeemed. If a
digital good was purchased in error and has not yet been redeemed, customers
may request a refund within 14 days of purchase by contacting support.

Perishable goods, including fresh food and flowers, are only eligible for a
refund if the item arrived damaged or spoiled. Customers must report damage
within 48 hours of delivery, along with photographic evidence, to qualify.

Subscription plans may be cancelled at any time from the account settings
page. Cancelling a subscription stops future billing but does not
automatically refund the current billing period; prorated refunds for the
current period are only issued if the cancellation is due to a service
outage lasting more than 24 continuous hours, as documented in our service
level agreement.

International orders are subject to the same 30-day refund window, but
customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless the item was
defective or misrepresented in the original listing. Customs duties and
import taxes paid at the time of delivery are non-refundable under any
circumstance.
