Shipping Policy

Standard shipping within the continental service area takes 3 to 5 business
days from the time an order is confirmed. Expedited shipping, available for
an additional fee, reduces this window to 1 to 2 business days for orders
placed before 2 PM local time on a business day.

Orders over $75 qualify for free standard shipping automatically at
checkout; no promo code is required. This threshold does not apply to
expedited or international shipping options, which are always charged at
the listed rate regardless of order total.

Once an order has shipped, customers receive a tracking link by email.
Packages that show as delivered but were not received by the customer
should be reported within 7 days; after this window, the carrier's
liability window closes and the platform cannot guarantee a replacement
or refund for the missing package.

Shipping delays caused by weather, customs holds, or carrier disruptions
are outside the platform's control and do not qualify for automatic
compensation. However, if a delay exceeds 10 business days beyond the
original estimate, customers may request a shipping fee refund by
contacting support with their order number.

Address changes can only be made before an order enters the "processing"
stage in the fulfillment system. Once an order is marked as processing,
the shipping address is locked and cannot be modified, even if the
customer contacts support immediately.
